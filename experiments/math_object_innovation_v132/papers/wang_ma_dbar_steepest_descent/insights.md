# Wang-Ma Dbar Steepest Descent

## Pass 1

- Wang and Ma extend the nonlinear steepest-descent workflow to oscillatory
  Riemann-Hilbert problems generated by the mKdV hierarchy with weighted
  Sobolev initial data.
- Lens opening uses a nonanalytic interpolation and the Cauchy-Green formula.
  This produces a mixed RHP/dbar problem: the pure RHP supplies the local model,
  while a two-dimensional singular-integral problem carries interpolation
  error.
- Their oscillatory-region workflow separates an `O(t^-1)` pure-RHP
  approximation error from an `O(t^-3/4)` dbar error, giving the latter as the
  dominant remainder. They also prove an `O(t^-1)` fast-decay regime and a
  Painleve-II-hierarchy transition regime.
- The method handles several simple stationary points by localization, phase
  reduction, and separated contributions before reconstruction.

## Deepest current insights

- The dbar extension supplies a useful certificate shape for weaker regularity:
  exact local model plus explicitly bounded interpolation defect. This is more
  relevant to ZenoDEX than the mKdV solution formula itself.
- The current Bernstein/Tau lane can adopt the same accounting discipline
  without implementing Riemann-Hilbert numerics: keep model proof, defect
  proof, interaction budget, and reconstruction bound as distinct receipt
  fields.
- The strongest new proof target is the error-budget compositor. It is small,
  runtime-relevant, and does not depend on importing the paper's analytic
  machinery into consensus code.

## Pass 2: exact-rational receipt bridge

- `approximation_defect_receipt.py` now enforces the exact typed budget law,
  canonical rational strings, finite interval coverage, adjacent overlap
  agreement, and a SHA-256 binding over the receipt body.
- The built-in replay accepts one valid cover and returns `UNKNOWN` for missing
  region, underestimated defect, omitted interaction, and overlap mismatch
  witnesses.
- Lean proves componentwise budget monotonicity, local margin gluing,
  finite-cover lifting, and a counterexample showing that margin dominance is
  load-bearing.
- Certificate identifiers remain opaque upstream assumptions. The executable
  bridge verifies arithmetic and composition, not the analytic facts named by
  those identifiers.
